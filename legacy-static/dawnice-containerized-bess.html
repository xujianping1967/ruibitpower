FAQ

Questions before choosing this product.

Which container model should I choose?

ESS500-1045 and ESS1000-2089 represent different power and energy levels. Selection depends on load, grid connection and project target.

Is it suitable for microgrids?

Yes, containerized BESS is commonly used for microgrids, industrial parks and solar storage projects.

What site details are needed?

Foundation, access, ambient temperature, grid voltage, PCS/EMS requirements and fire-safety requirements.
