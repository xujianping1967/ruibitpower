FAQ

Questions before choosing this product.

When should I choose high voltage instead of 51.2V?

High-voltage batteries are often used for larger homes and hybrid inverters designed around HV battery input.

Can the stack be expanded?

Yes, within the supported module and cluster limits, but the final design must match inverter requirements.

Is it suitable outdoors?

The product family is listed with IP65 protection, but installation environment and local codes still need review.
