FAQ

Questions before choosing this product.

Is BS09-112-D-P a complete AC BESS?

It is presented as a DC-side outdoor C&I battery system. PCS and EMS scope should be confirmed for the project.

Can multiple cabinets be paralleled?

Dawnice materials indicate up to 10 units in parallel; final design depends on PCS and site architecture.

What should EPCs verify?

Foundation, clearance, PCS matching, communication and fire-safety requirements.
