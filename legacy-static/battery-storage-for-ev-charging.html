Battery Storage for EV Charging Stations

BESS can help charging operators add power capacity, reduce peak demand and support solar charging when grid connection is limited or expensive.

Fast charging sites can create sharp demand peaks. If the grid transformer cannot support those peaks, operators may face expensive grid upgrades or long approval timelines. Battery storage can act as a buffer between the grid, solar system and chargers.

How storage helps

  • Charges during lower-demand periods or from solar generation.
  • Discharges during charging peaks to reduce transformer pressure.
  • Supports backup power for site controls and selected chargers.
  • Improves solar self-consumption at charging locations.

Key design inputs

EV charging storage should be sized from charger power, expected charging sessions, grid limit, solar capacity, tariff structure and required resilience. A site with several fast chargers needs a different configuration than a destination charger with rooftop PV.

Recommended systems

Outdoor cabinet ESS, liquid-cooled C&I ESS or containerized BESS are typical starting points depending on power level and space. EMS strategy matters because charging behavior changes throughout the day.

Project note: provide charger count, charger power, transformer capacity and daily charging profile before requesting BESS sizing.
