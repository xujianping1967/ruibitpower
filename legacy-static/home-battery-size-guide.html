5kWh, 10kWh or 20kWh Home Battery: How to Choose

The right home battery size depends on essential loads, daily consumption, solar generation and how long you want backup power to last.

Home battery selection should begin with the loads you want to support. A small apartment may only need lights, router and refrigeration backup. A villa or high-consumption household may need a larger battery bank and a stronger hybrid inverter.

Typical sizing logic

  • 5kWh: essential loads, short backup window or small solar systems.
  • 10kWh: common household backup and evening solar self-consumption.
  • 20kWh: larger homes, longer outages or higher energy independence goals.

Do not size by battery capacity alone

Check inverter power, surge load, usable battery depth, solar input, installation space and whether the system is low-voltage or high-voltage. A 20kWh battery may not run every appliance if inverter output is too small.

Information to prepare

List daily kWh usage, critical appliances, backup hours, solar size and inverter model. Ruibit Power can use those details to recommend a Dawnice wall-mounted, stackable or all-in-one home ESS configuration.

Practical rule: define essential loads first, then decide whether the goal is emergency backup, solar self-use or whole-home resilience.
