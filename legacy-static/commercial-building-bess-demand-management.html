Commercial Building BESS for Demand Management

Battery storage can help commercial buildings reduce peak demand, use more solar energy and support selected loads during outages.

Commercial buildings often have predictable daily peaks from HVAC, elevators, lighting, refrigeration or tenant loads. If electricity tariffs include demand charges or time-of-use pricing, a BESS can improve energy cost control.

Typical operating strategies

  • Discharge during peak demand windows.
  • Store rooftop solar energy for evening use.
  • Support essential building loads during outages.
  • Coordinate with generator or grid controls where required.

What to size first

Start with 15-minute or hourly load data. Identify the peak level, peak duration and how often it occurs. Then compare the value of peak reduction with the battery and PCS capacity required.

Product starting points

For many commercial buildings, an outdoor cabinet ESS or all-in-one C&I ESS is easier to deploy than a containerized system. Larger campuses may require multiple cabinets or containerized BESS.

Buyer tip: a commercial BESS should be evaluated against the tariff and load curve, not only against product capacity.
