Adding a Home Battery to Rooftop Solar

A home battery can increase solar self-consumption and provide backup power, but the right design depends on inverter type, loads and local grid rules.

Many homes generate excess solar power during the day and buy electricity back from the grid in the evening. A home battery stores part of that daytime generation for later use.

Check the existing inverter

If the home already has solar, confirm whether the inverter supports battery connection. Some projects need a hybrid inverter replacement, while others can use an AC-coupled battery approach.

Define backup scope

Whole-home backup requires more inverter power and battery capacity than essential-load backup. List the appliances that must keep running during an outage before choosing system size.

Consider export limits

In some markets, grid export limits affect how much solar can be sent to the grid. A battery can improve self-consumption, but control settings should match local requirements.

Installer note: provide solar inverter brand, PV size, daily consumption and backup load list before selecting a battery model.
