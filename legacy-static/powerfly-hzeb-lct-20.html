FAQ

Questions before choosing this product.

Why is HZEB-LCT-20 ground-mounted?

The larger capacity and weight make floor placement more suitable than a small wall-mounted battery.

What kind of home fits this model?

Large homes, villas and customers needing longer backup time are better fits.

What should installers verify?

Floor loading, clearance, inverter current limits and communication compatibility.
