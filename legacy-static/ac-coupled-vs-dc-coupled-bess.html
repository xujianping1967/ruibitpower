AC-Coupled vs DC-Coupled BESS

Coupling architecture affects solar storage efficiency, retrofit complexity, control strategy and equipment selection.

Solar + storage projects can connect the battery system on the AC side or the DC side. The right approach depends on whether the site is new or existing, what inverter equipment is already installed and how the system will operate.

AC-coupled storage

AC-coupled systems connect battery storage through an inverter or PCS on the AC side. They are often easier for retrofit projects because existing PV inverters can remain in place.

DC-coupled storage

DC-coupled systems connect storage closer to the PV DC bus. They can reduce conversion steps in some designs, but equipment integration and controls must be planned carefully.

How to decide

  • Retrofit or new-build project.
  • Existing inverter type and grid connection.
  • Solar export limits and curtailment.
  • Battery charging strategy.
  • Control and metering requirements.
Ruibit Power recommendation: share the solar inverter model, single-line diagram and grid connection limits before choosing AC-coupled or DC-coupled storage.
