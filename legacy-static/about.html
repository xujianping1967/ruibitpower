About

Ruibit Power is an International Energy Storage Solution Partner

We help global customers select, configure and source Dawnice-based LiFePO4 battery systems for residential, commercial and industrial applications.

Role

More than a product distributor.

Ruibit Power acts as a solution and export partner for Dawnice energy storage systems. Our work focuses on application review, product matching, quotation support, technical documentation, export delivery coordination and after-sales communication.

This positioning keeps the brand clear: Dawnice provides the product technology and manufacturing ecosystem; Ruibit Power helps international customers turn those product lines into practical energy storage configurations.

How we work

A clear process for international customers.

Project requirement

Customer shares application type, country, target capacity and backup needs.

System matching

Ruibit Power helps select the battery, inverter, PCS or BESS product family.

Technical review

Datasheets, diagrams, configuration notes and quotation materials are prepared.

Export coordination

Documentation, packing and shipping communication are coordinated for delivery.

Installation guidance

Remote technical communication supports installers and project teams.

After-sales support

Documentation and service communication continue after delivery.

Partnership clarity
