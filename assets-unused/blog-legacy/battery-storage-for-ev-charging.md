## Why this topic matters

Energy storage buyers often compare model numbers before the project requirements are clear. A better starting point is the application: backup power, self-consumption, demand management, EV charging support, or PV hybrid operation.

## What to check first

Confirm the site load profile, target backup duration, PV capacity, installation environment, grid rules, and preferred communication or monitoring requirements. These details decide whether a residential battery, cabinet system, or containerized BESS is the right direction.
