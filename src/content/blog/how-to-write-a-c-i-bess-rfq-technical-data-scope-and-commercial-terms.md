<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="2lsul4" data-start="76" data-end="128">A Good RFQ Makes the Quotations Boring to Compare</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="130" data-end="523"><strong data-start="130" data-end="523">A C&amp;I BESS RFQ should define three things before asking for a price: what the system must accomplish, exactly what the supplier must provide, and how performance will be accepted. At minimum, specify site/load data, required kW and kWh, operating modes, grid conditions, supply scope, standards, communications, FAT/SAT requirements, warranty, delivery boundary, and commercial milestones.</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="130" data-end="523"><img src="/assets/images/blog/how-to-write-a-c-i-bess-rfq-technical-data-scope-and-commercial-terms/1_751.jpg" alt="How to Write a C&I BESS RFQ: Technical Data, Scope, and Commercial Terms" width="400" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="525" data-end="751">DOE's current BESS procurement resources follow the same principle: technical requirements should be defined during procurement rather than left for clarification after supplier selection.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="753" data-end="810">I know an RFQ needs work when its technical section says:</p>
<blockquote data-start="812" data-end="851">
<p dir="auto" data-start="814" data-end="851">Please quote 500 kWh commercial BESS.</p>
</blockquote>
<p dir="auto" data-start="853" data-end="893">The supplier knows the battery capacity.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="895" data-end="935">Almost everything else is still unknown.</p>
<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="1qky086" data-start="937" data-end="985">Give Suppliers the Problem Before the Product</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="987" data-end="1046">Instead of starting with a cabinet size, describe the duty.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1048" data-end="1060">For example:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1062" data-end="1333"><strong data-start="1062" data-end="1078">Application:</strong> factory peak shaving + backup<br data-start="1108" data-end="1111"><strong data-start="1111" data-end="1135">Maximum site demand:</strong> 780 kW<br data-start="1142" data-end="1145"><strong data-start="1145" data-end="1167">Target grid limit:</strong> 600 kW<br data-start="1174" data-end="1177"><strong data-start="1177" data-end="1202">Critical backup load:</strong> 120 kW<br data-start="1209" data-end="1212"><strong data-start="1212" data-end="1241">Required backup duration:</strong> 60 minutes<br data-start="1252" data-end="1255"><strong data-start="1255" data-end="1271">Existing PV:</strong> 300 kWp<br data-start="1279" data-end="1282"><strong data-start="1282" data-end="1291">Grid:</strong> 400 V, 50 Hz<br data-start="1304" data-end="1307"><strong data-start="1307" data-end="1323">Transformer:</strong> 1,000 kVA</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1335" data-end="1411">Now Supplier A and Supplier B are at least trying to solve the same problem.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1413" data-end="1621">For peak-shaving projects, attach <strong data-start="1447" data-end="1488">15-minute or finer interval load data</strong> where available. For backup, include the critical-load schedule. For PV integration, provide PV inverter and generation information.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1623" data-end="1715">A monthly bill alone cannot show whether a 180 kW peak lasts fifteen minutes or three hours.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1717" data-end="1770">That difference can change the battery substantially.</p>
<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="64i2lr" data-start="1772" data-end="1831">Separate kW From kWh—and Define the Measurement Boundary</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1833" data-end="1877">Suppose the project specification calls for:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1879" data-end="1893"><strong data-start="1879" data-end="1893">250 kW PCS</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1895" data-end="1914"><strong data-start="1895" data-end="1914">500 kWh battery</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1916" data-end="1960">I would immediately add several definitions.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1962" data-end="1973">Is 500 kWh:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1975" data-end="2004"><strong data-start="1975" data-end="2004">nominal/nameplate energy?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="2006" data-end="2027"><strong data-start="2006" data-end="2027">usable DC energy?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="2029" data-end="2061"><strong data-start="2029" data-end="2061">guaranteed usable AC energy?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="2063" data-end="2173">Likewise, is 250 kW continuous at the expected site temperature and SoC, or merely a nominal converter rating?</p>

<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="1rhm10g" data-start="3056" data-end="3112">Scope Is Where the Cheapest Bid Usually Changes Price</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3114" data-end="3170">Before looking at the total, I would mark every item as:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3172" data-end="3226"><strong data-start="3172" data-end="3226">Included / Excluded / Optional / By EPC / By Owner</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3228" data-end="3240">Do that for:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3242" data-end="3259">battery cabinets;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3261" data-end="3265">PCS;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3267" data-end="3279">BMS and EMS;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3281" data-end="3304">HVAC or liquid cooling;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3306" data-end="3337">fire detection and suppression;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3339" data-end="3356">AC/DC protection;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3358" data-end="3385">transformer and switchgear;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3387" data-end="3416">metering and SCADA interface;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3418" data-end="3440">communication gateway;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3442" data-end="3449">cables;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3451" data-end="3471">foundation drawings;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3473" data-end="3480">spares;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3482" data-end="3486">FAT;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3488" data-end="3506">commissioning/SAT;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3508" data-end="3517">training;</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3519" data-end="3534">remote support.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3536" data-end="3553">Imagine two bids:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3555" data-end="3579"><strong data-start="3555" data-end="3579">Supplier A: $205,000</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3581" data-end="3605"><strong data-start="3581" data-end="3605">Supplier B: $178,000</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3607" data-end="3724">Then you discover A includes commissioning, EMS, spare parts and the transformer interface while B excludes all four.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3726" data-end="3785">The $27,000 difference was never purely a price difference.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3787" data-end="3817">It was a <strong data-start="3796" data-end="3816">scope difference</strong>.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="3819" data-end="4042">DOE procurement guidance similarly treats hardware, controls, technical specifications, commissioning, interconnection, and related project responsibilities as procurement-stage issues.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4044" data-end="4180">For a Ruibit/Dawnice RFQ, I would therefore ask for a line-by-line compliance and scope response instead of accepting one bundled price.</p>
<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="1iifs9e" data-start="4182" data-end="4213">Communications Need an Owner</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4215" data-end="4259">I would include a simple interface schedule:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4261" data-end="4274"><strong data-start="4261" data-end="4274">BMS ↔ PCS</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4276" data-end="4289"><strong data-start="4276" data-end="4289">PCS ↔ EMS</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4291" data-end="4311"><strong data-start="4291" data-end="4311">EMS ↔ site meter</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4313" data-end="4332"><strong data-start="4313" data-end="4332">EMS ↔ SCADA/BMS</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4334" data-end="4377"><strong data-start="4334" data-end="4377">Remote monitoring ↔ cloud/local network</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4379" data-end="4426">Then specify required protocols where relevant:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4428" data-end="4446"><strong data-start="4428" data-end="4446">Modbus TCP/RTU</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4448" data-end="4455"><strong data-start="4448" data-end="4455">CAN</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4457" data-end="4469"><strong data-start="4457" data-end="4469">Ethernet</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4471" data-end="4486"><strong data-start="4471" data-end="4486">digital I/O</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4488" data-end="4596">and the required data points, alarms, historian access, time synchronization and cybersecurity requirements.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4598" data-end="4631">But the most important column is:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4633" data-end="4654"><strong data-start="4633" data-end="4654">Responsible Party</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4656" data-end="4768">Because "Modbus supported" does not tell me who must make two devices actually communicate during commissioning.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4770" data-end="4896">If the battery supplier, PCS vendor and EPC each assume somebody else owns integration, the owner eventually owns the problem.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4770" data-end="4896"><img src="/assets/images/blog/how-to-write-a-c-i-bess-rfq-technical-data-scope-and-commercial-terms/1_546.jpg" alt="How to Write a C&I BESS RFQ: Technical Data, Scope, and Commercial Terms" width="400" height="400" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></p>
<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="1hk9qux" data-start="4898" data-end="4963">FAT and SAT Belong in the RFQ, Not in an Email Before Shipment</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="4965" data-end="4978">Do not write:</p>
<blockquote data-start="4980" data-end="5007">
<p dir="auto" data-start="4982" data-end="5007">Factory testing required.</p>
</blockquote>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5009" data-end="5038">Define what acceptance means.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5040" data-end="5081">Depending on the project, FAT may verify:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5083" data-end="5113"><strong data-start="5083" data-end="5113">BMS/PCS/EMS communications</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5115" data-end="5145"><strong data-start="5115" data-end="5145">charge/discharge operation</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5147" data-end="5172"><strong data-start="5147" data-end="5172">protection and alarms</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5174" data-end="5205"><strong data-start="5174" data-end="5205">thermal-management controls</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5207" data-end="5225"><strong data-start="5207" data-end="5225">emergency stop</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5227" data-end="5248"><strong data-start="5227" data-end="5248">remote monitoring</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5250" data-end="5289"><strong data-start="5250" data-end="5289">documentation and firmware versions</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5291" data-end="5364">SAT should prove the installed system performs the purchased application.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5366" data-end="5436">If the project was bought for peak shaving, test peak-shaving control.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5438" data-end="5492">If it was bought for backup, test the backup sequence.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5494" data-end="5594">If the contract guarantees usable energy, define the measurement boundary and acceptance conditions.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5596" data-end="5873">DOE's BESS technical specification resource is explicitly intended as a customizable procurement template, which is a useful reminder that acceptance criteria should be project-specific rather than copied blindly from another installation.</p>
<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="1smzpvj" data-start="5875" data-end="5906">Make Payment Follow Evidence</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5908" data-end="5964">Commercial terms should reinforce the technical process.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5966" data-end="5994">I prefer milestones such as:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="5996" data-end="6028"><strong data-start="5996" data-end="6028">technical agreement approved</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6030" data-end="6049">→ <strong data-start="6032" data-end="6049">design freeze</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6051" data-end="6069">→ <strong data-start="6053" data-end="6069">FAT accepted</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6071" data-end="6104">→ <strong data-start="6073" data-end="6104">shipping documents approved</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6106" data-end="6120">→ <strong data-start="6108" data-end="6120">delivery</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6122" data-end="6154">→ <strong data-start="6124" data-end="6154">SAT/commissioning accepted</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6156" data-end="6230">The percentages depend on the transaction, but the principle is important.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6232" data-end="6342">If nearly all payment is released before FAT, the buyer has very little leverage when FAT discovers a problem.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6344" data-end="6370">The RFQ should also state:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6372" data-end="6384"><strong data-start="6372" data-end="6384">Incoterm</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6386" data-end="6399"><strong data-start="6386" data-end="6399">lead time</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6401" data-end="6446"><strong data-start="6401" data-end="6446">packing and dangerous-goods documentation</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6448" data-end="6476"><strong data-start="6448" data-end="6476">insurance responsibility</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6478" data-end="6508"><strong data-start="6478" data-end="6508">commissioning/travel costs</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6510" data-end="6533"><strong data-start="6510" data-end="6533">warranty start date</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6535" data-end="6559"><strong data-start="6535" data-end="6559">performance warranty</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6561" data-end="6588"><strong data-start="6561" data-end="6588">spare-parts obligations</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6590" data-end="6610"><strong data-start="6590" data-end="6610">service response</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6612" data-end="6641"><strong data-start="6612" data-end="6641">software/firmware support</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6643" data-end="6707">and responsibility for freight and labor during warranty claims.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6709" data-end="6794">A ten-year BESS deserves more commercial definition than "10-year warranty included."</p>
<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="cfagxq" data-start="6796" data-end="6862">The RFQ Is Finished When Three Suppliers Can Quote the Same Job</h2>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6864" data-end="6880">That is my test.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6882" data-end="6986">If three suppliers can interpret the document as three different BESS projects, the RFQ is not finished.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="6988" data-end="7050">A useful Ruibit/Dawnice inquiry should let engineering answer:</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7052" data-end="7078"><strong data-start="7052" data-end="7078">What must the BESS do?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7080" data-end="7111"><strong data-start="7080" data-end="7111">Under what site conditions?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7113" data-end="7158"><strong data-start="7113" data-end="7158">What equipment and services are included?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7160" data-end="7205"><strong data-start="7160" data-end="7205">What evidence must accompany the product?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7207" data-end="7253"><strong data-start="7207" data-end="7253">How will FAT and SAT determine acceptance?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7255" data-end="7302"><strong data-start="7255" data-end="7302">Where does each party's responsibility end?</strong></p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7304" data-end="7370">Once those questions have fixed answers, price becomes meaningful.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7372" data-end="7478">The purpose of a C&amp;I BESS RFQ is not to tell the manufacturer how to design every busbar and control loop.</p>
<p dir="auto" data-start="7480" data-end="7602" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">It is to make it difficult for two suppliers to quote different scopes and still pretend they are offering the same thing.</p>

<h2 dir="auto" data-section-id="1xvwnkw" data-start="0" data-end="7">FAQs</h2>
<h3 dir="auto" data-section-id="1h6rmbi" data-start="9" data-end="70">1. What information should be included in a C&amp;I BESS RFQ?</h3>
<p dir="auto" data-start="72" data-end="330">Include <strong data-start="80" data-end="329">site and interval load data, required kW and kWh, operating objectives, grid and transformer information, environmental conditions, system architecture, communications, certification requirements, FAT/SAT criteria, warranty, and commercial terms</strong>.</p>
<h3 dir="auto" data-section-id="g85hq0" data-start="332" data-end="399">2. Why should kW and kWh be specified separately in a BESS RFQ?</h3>
<p dir="auto" data-start="401" data-end="588"><strong data-start="401" data-end="432">kW defines power capability</strong>, while <strong data-start="440" data-end="484">kWh defines energy capacity and duration</strong>. Buyers should also clarify whether kWh refers to nameplate, usable DC, or guaranteed usable AC energy.</p>
<h3 dir="auto" data-section-id="47xsft" data-start="590" data-end="662">3. How should buyers compare the scope of different BESS quotations?</h3>
<p dir="auto" data-start="664" data-end="878">Create an <strong data-start="674" data-end="728">Included / Excluded / Optional / By EPC / By Owner</strong> matrix covering batteries, PCS, EMS, cooling, fire protection, transformer, switchgear, communications, commissioning, training, spares, and support.</p>
<h3 dir="auto" data-section-id="1bodyos" data-start="880" data-end="942">4. Should FAT and SAT requirements be included in the RFQ?</h3>
<p dir="auto" data-start="944" data-end="1154">Yes. Define the required tests and measurable <strong data-start="990" data-end="1028">pass/fail criteria before purchase</strong>. FAT verifies the system before shipment, while SAT confirms that the installed BESS performs its required functions at site.</p>
<h3 dir="auto" data-section-id="1y5nzwm" data-start="1156" data-end="1210">5. What commercial terms should a BESS RFQ define?</h3>
<p dir="auto" data-start="1212" data-end="1455" data-is-last-node="" data-is-only-node="">Specify <strong data-start="1220" data-end="1454">payment milestones, Incoterm, lead time, shipping documentation, commissioning costs, warranty start date, performance obligations, spare parts, service response, software support, and responsibility for warranty freight and labor</strong>.</p>
